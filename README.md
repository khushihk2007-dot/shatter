# SHATTER

A premium cinematic glass-breaking web experience built for emotional release through interaction.

Upload an image.
Click. Crack. Shatter. Repeat.

Designed as a minimalist interactive art experience inspired by luxury editorial websites, Apple-level motion systems, cinematic WebGL interactions, and digital material simulation.

---

## LIVE DEMO

https://shatter-five.vercel.app/

---

# EXPERIENCE

SHATTER transforms any uploaded image into a floating sheet of premium digital glass.

Users can:

* upload an image
* click to fracture the surface
* generate realistic crack propagation
* shatter the image into cinematic glass fragments
* experience tactile physics-driven interactions

The project focuses on:

* premium motion design
* physically believable interactions
* cinematic visual feedback
* immersive glass material rendering
* emotionally satisfying destruction mechanics

---

The experience is designed to feel:

# CREATOR NOTES

SHATTER is not built as a traditional game.

It is designed as an interactive emotional interface —
a tactile digital experience where destruction becomes elegant, cinematic, and strangely calming.

The goal was to create something that feels less like breaking objects,
and more like interacting with fragile digital material floating in space.
